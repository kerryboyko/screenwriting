--- layout: default title: The Back Burner permalink: /ideas/ --- {% capture explain %} This is a collection of projects on my back burner: ideas that I haven't yet developed into finished works, but which are sufficiently formed to describe. Some of them still contain major craft flaws before they can even be considered for a treatment or pitch — some might have a compelling concept but not characters or a protagonist. Others might have a great setting but no real storyline. None of these should be considered ready for prime time. I'm sharing them here for two reasons. First, I believe this is the ideal stage to bring other writers into the creative process. It's an opportunity to explore possibilities, challenge assumptions, and test ideas before committing the time and effort required to write a first draft. Second, these projects offer a glimpse into the kinds of stories I'm interested in telling, the genres I enjoy working in, and the sort of writing I might bring to a collaboration.
