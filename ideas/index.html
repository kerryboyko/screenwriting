--- layout: default title: The Back Burner permalink: /ideas/ --- {% capture explain %} Not every idea arrives fully formed. Some start with a character, a situation, a question, or a premise that seems too interesting to leave alone. _The Back Burner_ is a collection of projects at various stages of development. Some have outlines, characters, and a reasonably clear direction. Others are little more than an intriguing premise and a vague suspicion that there's a story in there somewhere. None should be mistaken for finished work. They have unanswered questions, structural problems, and creative possibilities I haven't yet figured out how to exploit. That's part of the reason they're here. I'm sharing them partly as a glimpse into the kinds of stories that interest me, but also in the hope that something might catch another writer's imagination. Sometimes an idea needs a fresh perspective, a different set of instincts, or someone willing to ask the questions I've been avoiding.
