Another film to recommend because of excellence in craft is _My Cousin Vinny_. Like [_Back To The Future_]({{ '/viewing/back-to-the-future' | relative_url}}){: .marked_link}, every line and action in this movie accomplishes something. 

But _My Cousin Vinny_ does something magical. Without it becoming repetitive, without it becoming boring, _My Cousin Vinny_ constantly reminds you of the _stakes_ of the film. Like the arrow in the FedEx logo, you don't notice it until someone points it out. But every other scene tends to remind us what's at stake: The defendants are facing _the death penalty._ 

Additionally, it does something wonderful with those stakes: It continues to pile on _more_ as the story progresses. 

At first, it's just the fate of the boys. 

But soon, Vinny compounds the problem and has problems added for him. 

This is one of the most famous scenes in the film, and it's funny _precisely_ because Vinny is doing little more than listing all the things that can go wrong. Every item on that list is another reminder of just how much is riding on the outcome of this case.

_My Cousin Vinny_ uses its escalating stakes as a source of comedy, rather than allowing the comedy to _distract_ from those stakes. The audience laughs at the accumulation of problems, but the underlying danger never disappears. That's hard to pull off.
