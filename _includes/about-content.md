**Kerry Ann Burke** is a Dublin-based screenwriter, novelist, stand-up comic, and late-blooming, middle-aged transgender woman.

Recently having completed the coursework for an MA in Screenwriting for Film & Television at the Irish National Film School-IADT, she writes dark comedies, thrillers, and genre stories about survival, identity, and systems that go spectacularly wrong.

Before entering the MA program, she spent a decade as a journalist and a decade as a software engineer.

Her work combines sharp humor, social critique, and a fascination with how ordinary people adapt when the world refuses to cooperate.

Originally from the United States, she has lived in eight countries and now makes her home in Ireland.
