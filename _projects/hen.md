### Synopsis

Walking alone through the streets of London late at night, a lonely teenager stops outside a pub, captivated by a group of women celebrating a hen party. Watching their laughter, affection, and easy camaraderie, the teen closes his eyes and imagines himself among them, welcomed as one of the girls. But the fantasy is shattered when the pub landlord draws the curtains, leaving James alone in the cold, staring at a reflection she cannot bear to see.

### Writer's Note

*Hen* is one of my earliest works, which I've recently revisited after completing the coursework for my M.A. in Screenwriting. It began as a one-page screenwriting challenge.

Despite its brevity, I think it captures a moment I've experienced, and one that many transgender people will recognize: being excluded because of something you can't control. Wanting something that comes naturally to others, and being made to feel ashamed for wanting it.

I've been the teenager in *Hen* when I was a teenager myself, well into my 20s and 30s, and even into my early 40s.

I wanted to capture that *one moment*, because being transgender isn't just about clothes or hormones. It's about the experience of being excluded from things you desperately want out of life, often for reasons you don't yet understand.
