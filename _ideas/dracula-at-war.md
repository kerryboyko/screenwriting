In 1938, as Romania falls to fascism, Dracula wages a covert war against the regime and its Axis allies—not for justice, but to impress the king’s Jewish werewolf consort, Magda, before she’s forced to flee. He isn’t a hero—just a refined monster, and cruelty offends his taste. 

## Synopsis

In the crumbling heart of Eastern Europe, as fascism spreads like wildfire, an old monster finds himself drawn into a new kind of war. Dracula doesn’t fight for freedom, or for God, or even for the dead. He fights for Magda Lupescu—Jewish, a werewolf, and consort to King Carol II of Romania. 

He loves her. She will never love him. But when the Nazis arrive and the world tips toward genocide, Dracula decides: the amateurs must be stopped. 

He is no reformer. No revolutionary. Just an ancient predator with a code. And fascist cruelty? _Cruelty is for children._

### Comps

_Foyle's War_ meets _Dracula: Unbound_ and _Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Spy_

### Themes

What does it mean to be a monster in a world that creates worse ones? The burden of legacy—especially when that legacy is monstrous. Moral ambiguity in wartime: What atrocities can ever be justified? The cost of survival, and the weight of the choices we make to endure.  Navigating the “lesser evil” dilemma in an era where all choices reek of blood.
