_Going Broker_ is a high-stakes strategy game show where contestants begin as players, but the real goal is to become a broker — a recurring investor in the players that come after them. 

## Format

Each game begins with two randomly chosen contestants (three in the season opener) choosing one of several giant piggy banks, each containing a hidden cash amount. Before any piggy bank is opened, The Market offers every contestant a cash buyout. Take the buyout, and the contestant moves to Broker's Lounge with cash in hand, their piggy bank's value revealed and taken out of play. 

As the game progresses, piggy banks are revealed at random. When a contestant's piggy bank is opened, they leave the game and join the Broker's Lounge with whatever money was inside.

Once seated in the Broker's Lounge, players become brokers. Using their own money, brokers can negotiate deals with active contestants, purchase percentages of future winnings, offer guarantees, and invest in players still in the game. 

Every dollar spent on a bid is a dollar removed from a broker's bankroll, forcing brokers to decide whether a hidden opportunity is worth the risk. Every deal carries risk, reward, and the possibility of massive profit.

When all piggy banks are revealed, a new game begins with two new players randomly chosen from the contestant pool. 

Only three brokers can occupy the Broker's Lounge at any time. When a new broker arrives, an existing broker must cash out. If no one volunteers, the broker with the lowest cash-on-hand value must cash out — they can see their existing investments through to the end, but cannot propose in new ones. This creates constant pressure to grow, protect, and defend a broker's position.

Over the course of the season, successful brokers build their bankrolls and the eight contestants who cashed out with the most money earn one of eight places in the prestigious Hall of Brokers.

### Season Finale: The Hall of Brokers

The season's top brokers return for one final showdown.

A collection of giant piggy banks contains hidden cash values. Brokers use their accumulated bankrolls to bid for the right to claim and open piggy banks. The winning bid is deducted from the broker's total, while the contents of the piggy bank are added to it.

As piggy banks disappear, the value of the remaining piggy banks becomes easier to estimate, but the stakes become even higher.

One piggy bank remains unopened.

Its value is multiplied tenfold to create the Grand Prize.

When the final piggy bank is revealed, the broker with the highest bankroll wins the Grand Prize and is crowned the season's Ultimate Broker.

### Why It Works

_Going Broker_ combines the emotional tension of hidden-value games, the strategy of investing, and the long-term storytelling of reality competition shows. Contestants do not simply win money. They build wealth, make deals, take risks, and attempt to transform themselves from ordinary players into master brokers.

Because brokers remain in the game across multiple rounds, every deal creates new alliances, rivalries, and opportunities. A player who starts with very little can still rise to the top through superior judgment, while an early leader can lose everything through a single bad investment.

The hidden-value tension of _Deal or No Deal_, the investment negotiations of _Shark Tank_, the season-long advancement of _Survivor_, and the bankroll strategy of _High Stakes Poker_.
