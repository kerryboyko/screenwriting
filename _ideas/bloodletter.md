After centuries of being overshadowed by Dracula, Varney the Vampire claws his way into the modern world to find a writer who will finally tell his story—and save him from being erased from literary history forever.

## Synopsis

Once the _original_ literary vampire—serialized in the penny dreadfuls of the 1840s—Sir Francis Varney now lives in obscurity, nursing centuries of literary envy and existential malaise. He’s watched other vampires get movies, merch, musical numbers. Meanwhile, his “memoirs” were long, overwrought, and mostly used in Victorian lit courses to torture undergrads.

Dracula got the myth. Lestat got the merch. Edward got the memes. Varney? He got _footnotes_.

Now Varney’s back, trying to reclaim relevance in a publishing industry no one survives without a platform. He’s sending queries. Trying self-pub. Offering immortality to anyone who can ghostwrite with flair. (He’s _very_ dramatic.) But he’s not just asking to be remembered. He’s begging not to be erased. 

Also, he might kill you— But not _before_ you get him a book deal.


### Comps

_The Disaster Artist_ meets _What We Do In The Shadows_ and _Shadow of the Vampire_

### Themes

The curse of the mid-tier creator. Legacy, jealousy, and the absurdity of “voice.” The aching, vampiric desire to matter. “Who Lives, Who Dies, Who Tells Your Story.”

### Tagline
