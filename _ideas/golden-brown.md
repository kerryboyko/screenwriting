## Synopsis

Golden Brown combines the gritty realism of The Wire and Dopesick with werewolf mythology.

Liam Faelan is a philosophical werewolf enforcer for the Kilkenny Wolves, a criminal syndicate whose members are bound by blood, loyalty, and violence. Unlike his ruthless associates, Liam believes violence should serve a purpose, not an ego.

As he puts it: _"Violence is always my last resort. Because if it isn't, then I didn't resort to enough of it."_ 

When a mysterious new strain of heroin begins transforming overdosing addicts into savage monsters, Liam is tasked with tracing its source before the epidemic threatens the syndicate's profits and exposes the existence of werewolves.

With the help of a desperate pharmacology student and a trusted subordinate, Liam follows the drug through a criminal underworld of dealers, smugglers, and addicts. But as his investigation uncovers a conspiracy reaching into his own organization, Liam must prevent his increasingly impatient superiors from solving the crisis through indiscriminate slaughter.

At its heart, Golden Brown explores addiction, belonging, and the seductive power of violence in a world where the most dangerous monsters aren't necessarily supernatural.
