Facing a soul-market crash triggered by late-stage capitalism, a cabal of demon investors schemes to inflate human goodness long enough to sell high—before the bottom falls out of humanity.

## Synopsis

In Des Moines, Iowa—the insurance capital of the Midwest—a branch office of demonic traders handles _Negotiated Fate Transfers (NFTs)_: contracts for human souls, bundled into _Collateralized Damnation Obligations_ and sold on the infernal futures market.

But in the age of burnout, debt, and algorithmic despair, humans are arriving in hell already maxed out. Their souls? _Subprime._ The market? About to _crash_. And if that happens, hell freezes over—literally.

To avoid catastrophe, this team must temporarily improve human lives just enough to restore soul value. Not because they care. Because they’re holding the bag. And maybe, just maybe, they’re starting to like being accidentally good at it.

### Comps

_Lucifer_ meets _The Big Short_

### Themes

The commodification of suffering. Bureaucracy as the devil’s playground. Moral compromise for profit. The fine print of redemption. Unintentional resistance as market manipulation.
