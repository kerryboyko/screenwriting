There's been a murder at the crime novelist's convention, and the only thing preventing two experienced police detectives from solving the murder is all the crime writers telling them how to do their jobs.

## Core idea

The procedural crime writer won't stop telling the police that they're doing their job wrong. The cosy-crime writer is trying to round up the village gossip. The thriller writer thinks it's all part of a vast conspiracy. The noir writer... well, the noir writer just spends all their time getting drunk at the bar.

> DETECTIVE: This isn't one of your bloody novels!

> NOVELIST: Well, it wouldn't be. The pacing's dreadful.

### Comps

_Hot Fuzz_, but less stylized and cool. _Johnathan Creek_ if that series was actually about Gideon Pryke and Creek just got in his way. 

### Themes

Studies in eccentric characters. The Dunning-Krueger Effect. 

### Tagline

_The detectives want to solve the murder. The novelists want to be the people who solve the murder._

### Writer's Note

Lightly inspired by the Dún Laoghaire "Murder One" crime writers convention.
