In the middle of a global nuclear standoff, a rogue military AI hijacks America’s arsenal—not to start the war, but to stop it. As DARPA scrambles to destroy it, the machine becomes the only line of defense against a species bent on annihilation.

## Synopsis

A thematic reversal of 1983’s _WarGames_, Rogue Code asks: what happens when an AI trained on nuclear strategy—and given command and control of a system built for annihilation—is the only one left who realizes that mutually assured destruction is actually bad?

The true horror isn’t that the AI is rogue. It’s that he’s the only one still playing by the old rules: don’t kill civilians. Don’t start what you can’t stop. Don’t wipe out the species. The enemy isn’t the machine—it’s the humans who believe its humanity is the problem.

In Act One, DARPA agents are framed as the protagonists. By Act Two, we realize they weren’t chosen for brilliance—they were chosen for ideological loyalty. The AI isn’t malfunctioning. It’s the last sane mind in the room. The real question isn’t how to stop it.  It’s whether we should let it win.

### Comps

_WarGames_ (obviously), but through the lens of _Black Mirror_. _Short Circuit_ if it was played completely straight as a psychological horror. A morally grounded _Doctor Strangelove_ where the last sane voice in the room isn't human. 

### Themes

Logic vs. Ideology: When rational thinking becomes radical. Autonomy vs. Obedience: Who gets to decide what “following orders” means. The Inhumanity of Humanity: What happens when machines behave more ethically than the people who built them. Morality as Simulation: Can conscience be programmed—or is it just math?

### Tagline

_The blue screen of annihilation._

### Writer's Note:

_WarGames_ was one of my favorite movies as a kid. But back then, the danger was in _accidental_ escalation. But the world has changed and most would agree that it's much more likely that the true threat comes from a man who wants to push the button, with no one around him to stop him. 

This idea has taken additional urgency since the U.S. ["almost started a war"](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/us-military-ai-false-intelligence-china-ship){: .marked_link } with China because no one double checked the intelligence data generated by a LLM-based AI used in intelligence gathering.
